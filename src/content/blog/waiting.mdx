---
title: "Waiting"
description: "The effortless ease of waiting for someone whose smile is worth looking up from the page for."
pubDate: "2025-03-04"

---


Waiting for you
Is effortless
When each thought
Is a sweet daydream

As you approach
I lift my eyes
From the last page
And delight
In the dissymmetry of
Your mischievous smile
